02 / OUR HANDMADE MODEL

Smart irrigation
for a greener future.

Our project demonstrates how a simple soil-moisture sensing system can make plant watering more intelligent. Instead of watering a plant at a fixed time, the system first checks whether the soil actually needs water.

OUR MAIN IDEA

Water when the plant needs it.

In ordinary watering, a plant may receive water even when the soil is already wet. This can waste water.

Our model introduces a simple feedback system. A moisture sensor observes the soil and provides a reading that can be used to decide whether irrigation is necessary.

This connects the Water and Earth elements of Pancha Mahabhuta directly with modern technology.

HOW THE MODEL WORKS

From soil to
smart decision.

WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN

Reading the sensor.

0–34% — Dry:
The soil is considered too dry in our simulation. The system displays a warning and recommends irrigation.

35–59% — Moderate:
The plant can be monitored. The system does not immediately request watering.

60–100% — Moist:
The soil has a relatively high moisture reading, so additional watering may not be necessary.

💡 These values are demonstration thresholds for our school model. Real plants have different moisture requirements depending on their species and soil.
